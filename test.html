004 / THE DIRECTION, NOT A DEADLINE

Try. Build. Rethink. Repeat.

A startup isn't a straight line. You learn, change your mind, and keep moving.

01

02

Ship a first version.

Get something useful into someone's hands.

03

Test what happens.

Watch behavior. Ask questions. Learn from evidence.

04

Change the product, customer, or approach.

05

Win paying customers and earn their return.
